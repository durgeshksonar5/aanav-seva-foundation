The Challenge

Child malnutrition and lack of daily access to healthy meals lead to developmental delays, weak immunity, and high school dropout rates. Special-needs and underprivileged children in Pune's municipal schools are extremely vulnerable, requiring systematic daily nutritional interventions.

Our Intervention

Aanav Seva Foundation implements the Poshan Aahar Program—delivering fresh, hot, and hygienic meals directly to special-needs children and municipal schools daily. The meals are curated by child nutrition experts to supply correct daily portions of vitamins, proteins, and essential nutrients.

Daily delivery of hot, balanced tiffins
Menus designed by child nutrition experts
Central kitchen adhering to high hygiene
Active tracking of child growth metrics
