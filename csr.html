Why Partner With Us

Delivering Compliance, Execution, & Measurable ESG Impact

Aanav Seva Foundation is a community-first, legally registered Section 8 Non-Profit organization in Pune, Maharashtra. We act as a trusted implementation partner for corporate CSR initiatives under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013. We bridge the gap between corporate CSR resources and marginalized grassroots communities.

We design, execute, and monitor social development projects in women's health, menstrual hygiene, child nutrition, environmental carbon offsets (bamboo plantations), and community livelihood. By choosing Aanav, you partner with a professional, audit-ready implementation team committed to zero leakage, complete operational transparency, and verifiable indicators.

Professional Execution

Project Implementation Process

We implement a structured, transparent, and step-by-step lifecycle for every corporate CSR project. This ensures seamless alignment between donor objectives and grassroots reality.

01

Needs Assessment

We perform localized demographic audits, consulting village panchayats and municipal schools to identify critical social gaps.

02

Baseline Survey

We measure pre-intervention parameters, including sanitation access, nutrition levels, or tree count, to establish clear starting metrics.

03

Project Planning

We map target areas, allocate resource budgets, and structure timeline milestones in line with the client's corporate ESG matrix.

04

Community Mobilisation

We conduct localized trust-building workshops, enlist village leaders, and establish peer-educator networks for sustainable adoption.

05

Implementation

We deploy medical personnel, build nutrition centers, distribute hygiene kits, or plant bamboo saplings with zero leakage.

06

Monitoring

Our field officers use geo-tagged photography and central databases to track daily execution logs and distribution metrics.

07

Impact Assessment

We conduct post-campaign surveys to evaluate adaptation rates, health improvements, or tree survival rates against the baseline.

08

CSR Reporting

We deliver comprehensive audit portfolios, including CA-certified utilization certificates and GPS-tracked impact metrics.

Transparency Standards

Institutional Monitoring & Reporting Framework

To provide corporate donors with complete peace of mind, we eliminate operational ambiguity by integrating systematic oversight at every stage:

  • GPS & Geo-tagged Field Interventions

    Every distribution camp and plantation site is tagged with GPS coordinates and timestamps, proving real-world project deployment.

  • CA-Certified Utilization Certificates

    We provide detailed ledger records and Chartered Accountant utilization sheets for seamless audit compliance under Section 135 MCA rules.

  • Replicable & Scalable ESG Frameworks

    Our modular operational manuals allow us to replicate successful interventions across multiple blocks and districts rapidly, scaling impact.

Credibility Anchor

Flagship CSR Partnership: JSW Foundation MHM Initiative

Under our flagship corporate alignment with the **JSW Foundation**, Aanav Seva Foundation deployed structured Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) campaigns across Maharashtra's rural municipal schools.

This collaboration serves as a model of our operational efficiency. We successfully managed the procurement, logistics, rural awareness sessions, and distributed over **50,000+ hygiene products** across 20+ municipal schools with a verified 98% sustained adaptation rate.
