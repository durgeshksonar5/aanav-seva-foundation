The Challenge

Lack of access to clean sanitary napkins and deep-rooted taboos around menstrual health in rural Maharashtra leads to high school absenteeism (up to 25% absences) and high risks of reproductive tract infections. Without proper guidance, young girls and women undergo severe hygiene challenges during their monthly cycles.

Our Intervention

Aanav Seva Foundation implements the Aditi Program to deliver structured Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM) workshops, distribute free sanitary napkins to rural municipal schools, and build local networks of peer educators. In flagship partnership with JSW Foundation, we deploy resources and medical expertise at scale.

  • Free high-quality sanitary napkin distribution
  • Medical practitioner-led awareness workshops
  • Dispelling cultural myths and health taboos
  • Setting up safe school incinerator networks

Frequently asked questions

The program focuses on Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM), breaking local social taboos, conducting wellness workshops, and distributing free sanitary napkins to rural women in Maharashtra.

This initiative is supported by corporate CSR partners, including the JSW Foundation, along with dedicated healthcare volunteers and local community organizers.

To date, the program has reached and supported over 2,500 rural women with clean sanitary products and hygiene education.
