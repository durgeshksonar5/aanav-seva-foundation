CSR Implementation Partner

Grassroots Execution, Institutional Credibility

Aanav Seva Foundation is a community-first, legally registered Section 8 Non-Profit organization in Pune, Maharashtra. We act as a trusted implementation partner for corporate CSR initiatives under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, ensuring rigorous reporting, active auditing, and measurable social impact.

Our flagship collaboration with the JSW Foundation demonstrates our capacity to deploy structured menstrual hygiene management programs at scale, providing clean resources and training to thousands of beneficiaries across rural Maharashtra.
