The Challenge

Urban air pollution, rapid concrete expansion, and rising CO₂ emissions in Pune pose severe ecological threats to the city's green cover and health. The biodiversity zone in the Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) campus requires structured rehabilitation and high-oxygen releasing flora to act as a vital urban green lung.

Our Intervention

Aanav Seva Foundation implements the Kalpavruksha Program—an environmental sustainability campaign centered on planting high-oxygen producing Bamboo trees. We engage student communities, NSS volunteers, and environment enthusiasts to execute soil preparation, geo-tagged planting, and systematic watering schedules.

  • Selection of high carbon-absorbing Bamboo
  • Student and NSS volunteer mobilization
  • Long-term sapling care and monitoring
  • Promoting local environmental education

Frequently asked questions

Bamboo is one of the fastest-growing woody plants on earth. It releases up to 35% more oxygen than equivalent trees, features exceptional carbon sequestration capacity, and helps prevent soil erosion in Pune's university zones.

We have successfully established a model ecological zone containing over 300 bamboo saplings in the amenity and biodiversity sectors of the Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) campus.

We actively partner with local university students, NSS coordinators, and residential green clubs. Volunteers are trained in basic arboriculture and run weekly watering and maintenance schedules to ensure maximum sapling survival rates.
