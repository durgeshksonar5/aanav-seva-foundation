Execution Scope

We Implement CSR Projects In

Aanav Seva Foundation offers corporate partners structured, turn-key program execution modules across multiple critical social sectors.

Women's Health

Conducting rural diagnostic camps, gynecological check-ups, and maternal health programs, bridging healthcare access gaps.

Menstrual Hygiene

MHM awareness camps, distributing free high-quality sanitary napkins, and training local health workers in rural schools.

School Health

Conducting wellness audits, physical check-ups, eye clinics, and personal safety awareness sessions inside municipal schools.

Nutrition (Poshan)

Operating daily hot, hygienic tiffin services and nutrient supplements to address child malnutrition and improve school attendance.

Livelihood & Skills

Vocational training, tailorship modules, and financial literacy workshops to empower rural women and self-help groups (SHGs).

Environment & Plantations

Executing high-density bamboo plantations (Kalpavruksha model) to offset carbon footprints and optimize urban biodiversity zones.

Behaviour Change (BCC)

Driving community-led campaigns, puppet shows, street plays, and pamphlets to counter deep-rooted hygiene and health taboos.

Rural Health Outreach

Deploying mobile testing drives, generic medicine dispensaries, and health camps to distant hamlets lacking primary clinics.

WASH Programs

Upgrading rural school toilets, setting up sanitary incinerators, and installing RO water filtration units to promote hygiene.
